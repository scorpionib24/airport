Travel Guidelines

Before Travel
  • Ensure your passport and visas are valid.
  • Book your flight early and arrange transportation to the airport.
  • Check the allowed baggage weight.
  • Arrive at the airport well before your flight time.
During Travel
  • Keep your travel documents handy.
  • Follow security and safety instructions at the airport and on the plane.
  • Make sure you know your boarding gate.
  • Keep an eye on your personal belongings.
بعد السفر
  • Make sure you collect all your baggage from the carousel.
  • Check your passport and entry stamp upon arrival.
  • Follow customs instructions if required.
  • Arrange your transportation from the airport to your destination.

Special Assistance

Comprehensive Support for Passengers with Special Needs

We are committed to providing a comfortable and safe travel experience for everyone. If you require any type of special assistance, please notify us in advance or contact our team at the airport.

  • Assistance with airport mobility and wheelchairs
  • Support for elderly passengers and unaccompanied minors
  • Facilities for passengers with hearing or visual impairments
  • Priority during travel and security procedures
  • Escort service upon request
For requests or inquiries, please contact the Customer Service Desk or call: +249123456789

عن مطار الخرطوم الدولي

مطار الخرطوم الدولي هو البوابة الجوية الحديثة والرئيسية لجمهورية السودان، حيث يلتقي التراث العريق مع أحدث الخدمات لتوفير تجربة سفر استثنائية للمسافرين من جميع أنحاء العالم.

  • موقع استراتيجي في قلب العاصمة السودانية
  • خدمات متطورة ومرافق حديثة للمسافرين
  • فريق عمل محترف لخدمة الزوار
  • تجربة سفر آمنة ومريحة على مدار الساعة
